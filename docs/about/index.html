About

An archive kept by one researcher, for work that holds up after the fix has shipped.

I work on vulnerability research full time, mostly alone, mostly by reading source. The work splits between kernel and platform internals, desktop and mobile clients, and the cloud and identity surfaces that sit behind them.

Why the archive is slow

A finding is worth writing up when the fix is public and the reasoning can be shown end to end. Until then it stays unpublished. That keeps this archive thin for long stretches, and it keeps everything in it checkable.

What a writeup is for

Each one should let somebody retrace the investigation: enough context to follow the reasoning, enough detail to reproduce the observation, and a clear line between what was established and what is still a hypothesis.
