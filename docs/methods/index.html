04 / Research methods

Research methods

The principles this archive is built to follow.

01

Observe before assuming.

Start with a specific behavior or question. Keep a record of what was observed and what is still uncertain.

02

Make it reproducible.

Document the relevant environment, versions, inputs, and steps. Keep the evidence close to the claim it supports.

03

Separate evidence from inference.

Explain what the results establish, which interpretations remain possible, and where the investigation reaches its limits.

04

Publish with care.

Research in controlled, authorized environments. Handle vulnerability disclosure appropriately and remove private data before sharing.
